About Russia

Russia is the largest country in the world by area. It stretches across Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. The country is known for its historic cities, natural beauty and cultural traditions.

From the busy streets of Moscow to the peaceful landscapes of Lake Baikal, Russia offers many interesting places to discover.
